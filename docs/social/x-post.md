## Thread

**1/** · `docs/social/tysa-x.png`

> In a 2025 study, students who practiced math with ChatGPT scored 48% higher in practice, and 17% lower on the exam once it was taken away.
>
> Hand AI your thinking and your thinking decays.
>
> So I built the opposite: TYSA, Teach Yourself Anything 🧵
>
> #MetaAIHackathon

**2/**

> TYSA is a learning harness. It doesn't remove the struggle. It removes everything else: finding sources, planning, fact-checking.
>
> So all your effort goes into the material.

**3/** · `docs/assets/screenshot-probe.png`

> 1. Measure.
>
> Quick probes find the edge of what you know. It reads your answer, how sure you are, and your reasoning, typed or spoken.
>
> Two people can pick the same wrong answer for completely different reasons.

**4/** · `docs/assets/screenshot-plan.png`

> 2. Plan.
>
> A dependency map from what you already know to your goal. You see the whole plan, and confirm it or ask for changes, before anything is taught.

**5/** · `docs/assets/screenshot-teaching.png`

> 3. Teach.
>
> You predict each step first. Then it explains and checks that it landed. Confusions get repaired before anything is built on them, and the plan changes when your answers surprise it.

**6/** · `docs/assets/screenshot-home.png`

> Everything you learn becomes a knowledge graph: ideas, and the reasoning that connects them.
>
> Later lessons build on it, and practice draws problems from it. Hints never hand you the answer.
